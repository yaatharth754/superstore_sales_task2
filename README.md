# 🛒 Superstore Sales Analysis (SQL + Python)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes the Superstore Sales dataset using:
- Python (Pandas)
- SQLite (SQL Queries)
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Business Insights & KPI Analysis

The objective is to extract meaningful insights from transactional sales data and answer key business questions using SQL.

## 📊 Business Questions Answered

1️⃣ Top 5 Products by Revenue  
2️⃣ Monthly Sales Trend  
3️⃣ Revenue by Region  
4️⃣ Revenue by Customer Segment  
5️⃣ Average Shipping Time by Ship Mode  
6️⃣ Top 10 Customers by Revenue  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Revenue is concentrated among top-performing products.
- Sales exhibit seasonal fluctuations.
- Regional and segment-wise performance varies significantly.
- A small group of customers drives a major portion of revenue.
- Shipping efficiency impacts customer satisfaction.
